About the Founder

👋Hello! I'm Muhammad Ahmed, a passionate frontend developer and the creator of TDS CodeFx.

My journey into web development began in 2023, right after completing my 10th grade. During a 5–6 month break, I decided to invest my time in learning a valuable skill instead of wasting it.

Initially, I explored photo editing on mobile, but soon realized that most professional work required a computer. That’s when I discovered web development through YouTube — and everything changed.

At the beginning, coding felt confusing and overwhelming. I struggled to understand even the basics and almost gave up after watching a few tutorials. But then I made a strong decision:
“No matter what happens, I will learn this skill.”

Since I didn’t have access to a computer, I started learning and practicing entirely on my Android mobile using coding apps like TrebEdit.

Step by step, I learned HTML and CSS, and started building UI designs.
